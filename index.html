About Our Dog Matching Service

Welcome to our dog matching system! We're dedicated to helping you find the perfect canine companion that fits your lifestyle, living situation, and family needs.

How It Works

Our intelligent matching algorithm considers multiple factors to pair you with compatible dogs:

  • Your living space and environment
  • Activity level and exercise preferences
  • Experience with dogs
  • Family composition (children, other pets)
  • Grooming commitment
  • Training availability
  • Special needs (hypoallergenic requirements)

Research-Based Matching

Our system incorporates veterinary research and breed-specific knowledge to ensure responsible matches. For example, we account for the fact that puppies under 2 years old require significant time, training, and energy - and may not be suitable for apartment living without outdoor access.

Get Started

Click "Find Your Match" to take our comprehensive questionnaire, or browse "All Dogs" to see every available companion.

Remember: Every dog is unique! While our matching system provides guidance, we encourage you to meet potential matches in person and consider professional guidance from veterinarians and trainers.
